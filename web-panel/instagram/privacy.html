Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

First Class Inbox ("the App") is a customer messaging platform operated by First Class travel agency (Minsk, Belarus). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram Business account through Facebook Login, we access:

  • Instagram Business Profile — username, user ID, and profile picture (via instagram_business_basic)
  • Instagram Direct Messages — message content, sender name, and timestamps for conversations with your business account (via instagram_business_manage_messages)
  • Facebook Pages — list of Pages connected to your account (via pages_show_list)

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Display incoming Instagram Direct Messages in a unified inbox for travel agency managers
  • Enable managers to reply to customer inquiries directly from the App
  • Classify customer requests by type (e.g., flights, tours, visa support)
  • Send internal notifications to the team via Telegram
  • Check if the customer exists in the agency's CRM (U-ON.Travel)

3. Data Storage & Retention

  • We do not store Instagram messages permanently on our servers
  • Messages are fetched in real-time from the Instagram API and displayed in the browser session
  • We do not download, cache, or store media files (images, videos) from Instagram CDN
  • Session data is cleared when the user logs out

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties
  • Customer inquiry data may be forwarded to travel agency managers via internal Telegram notifications for the sole purpose of responding to the customer
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes

5. Data Security

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Facebook access tokens are stored securely and never exposed to end users
  • Access to the App is restricted to authorized travel agency staff

6. User Rights

You can at any time:

  • Disconnect your Instagram account by removing the App from your Facebook Business Settings
  • Request deletion of any data we may hold by contacting us
  • Revoke access through your Instagram or Facebook account settings

7. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below. We will process your request within 30 days. You may also remove app access directly from your Facebook Settings > Business Integrations.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
